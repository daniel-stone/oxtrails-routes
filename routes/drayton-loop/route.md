A short lap of the local byways and bridleways around **Drayton and Sutton Courtenay**. The route runs on a variety of earth, gravel and tarmac surfaces, and is non-technical and suitable for hybrid bikes and upwards. Parts of the route (particularly those west of the A34) can get a little muddy in winter and are best avoided after prolonged periods of rain.

## Route notes

The start follows NCN5 / Peep-O-Day Lane, but soon veers off through the gravel pits. The short section in Sutton Wick requires a little thoughtful navigation — cycle to the end of the road and keep heading left. Head to Sutton Courtenay and follow Mill Lane as it disappears and turns into a track towards Steventon. After navigating through Drayton, keep following the farm track out to the route of the Wilts and Berks Canal. As you turn off the track you'll follow the boundary of a grass field that can get very boggy. Once past this, the surface improves and you can use a concrete track and bridleways to find your way back to Mill Road in Abingdon.
