**This is a test fixture, not a rideable route.** It exists to show how the
site renders a GPX whose track is made of disjoint pieces.

The file holds ten separate stretches of Oxfordshire bridleway — near Claydon,
Chastleton, Godington, Middle Barton, Cumnor, Broughton Poggs, Sydenham,
Blewbury, Ashbury and Dunsden Green — with nothing joining them. The closest
two are about 20 km apart. Geometry is traced from OpenStreetMap ways tagged
`highway=bridleway`, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
